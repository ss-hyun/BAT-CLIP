Abstract

Decoding and interpreting naturalistic speech from the brain increasingly relies on alignment to pretrained speech and language representation spaces. However, current CLIP-style brain–speech alignment grounds neural activity to a single anchor modality—audio or text—despite the brain's inherently multimodal speech processing. This induces a trade-off: audio anchoring preserves temporal structure but weakens linguistic separability, while text anchoring captures semantics yet discards acoustic detail.

We propose BAT-CLIP, the first CLIP-style trimodal alignment framework for iEEG that jointly aligns neural embeddings to both pretrained audio and text anchors in a shared, frozen audio–text manifold. On the naturalistic Podcast benchmark, BAT-CLIP yields more robust representations than bimodal CLIP baselines. We also highlight the importance of using self-supervised foundation models for CLIP training.

Method

Fig. 1. Overview of BAT-CLIP. A trainable brain encoder fθ, initialized from the brain foundation model DIVER-1, maps neural windows to brain embeddings zB. Frozen AudioCLIP encoders provide audio anchors zA and text anchors zT. BAT-CLIP optimizes a trimodal contrastive objective LTotal to align neural embeddings with the joint audio–text representation space.

  • Alignment prior. The brain encoder is initialized from DIVER-1, an iEEG foundation model pretrained with self-supervised masked reconstruction, then adapted with a lightweight two-layer MLP projection to a shared d=1024 space with ℓ2 normalization. The pretrained weights act as a prior that curbs overfitting on the ~30 min of paired brain–audio–text data available for alignment.
  • Shared audio–linguistic space. Audio waveforms and transcripts are encoded by the pretrained AudioCLIP audio/text encoders, kept frozen so the brain encoder matches a stable, well-regularized manifold in the low-data regime.
  • Trimodal contrastive alignment. Symmetric Brain–Audio and Brain–Text cross-entropy losses are combined with equal weight into LTotal = ½(LBA + LBT), aligning brain embeddings to both anchors at once.
